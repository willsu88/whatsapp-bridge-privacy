Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy covers a WhatsApp messaging bridge (the "bridge"), a proof of concept built during Cresta's APAC build week. The bridge connects a WhatsApp test number to a Cresta AI agent so that a user can hold a text conversation with that agent over WhatsApp.

What the bridge does with your messages

  • It receives WhatsApp messages sent to the test number.
  • It forwards the text of those messages to a Cresta AI agent and returns the agent's reply to WhatsApp.
  • It does not store message content beyond what is held in memory while the request is being handled.
  • It does not sell, share or disclose message content to anyone other than the Cresta service that generates the reply.

Who can use it

The test number is a Meta-provided sandbox number. It accepts messages only from a small allowlist of phone numbers registered by the operator, and it can reply only to those numbers. It is not open to the public.

What is collected

The bridge logs the sender's WhatsApp identifier and the length of each message for operational debugging. Message text is not logged by default. No account, profile or contact data is collected, and no data is retained after the request completes.

Third parties

Messages are processed by Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, which delivers them to the bridge, and by Cresta's AI agent platform, which generates the reply. Each of those services handles data under its own privacy policy.

Changes

This is a short-lived proof of concept. This policy may be updated as the bridge changes, and the date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.
